FAQ

Common questions and answers, grouped by topic.

Basics

Where can I open PDF files from?

Use the file picker shown at launch to select a PDF from your device or iCloud Drive.

The menu or slider is not visible.

Tap the PDF image to toggle the toolbar, search bar, and slider.

How do I move between pages?

Swipe on the screen, or show the menu and use the slider at the bottom of the screen.

Viewing and Controls

Does it support right-to-left PDFs?

Yes. The app reads the PDF Direction and PageLayout settings and displays pages in the appropriate order for right-to-left or left-to-right reading.

Can I switch between single-page and two-page view?

Yes. You can change the view from the display settings, including how the cover page is handled.

Can I move around a zoomed PDF?

After pinching to zoom, long-press and drag to move the visible area. Double-tap to reset zoom.

What should I do if the display looks blurry?

Turn on High Quality or Sharpness in the settings. These options may increase rendering load on some devices.

Data and Security

Are PDF files sent outside the device?

PDF viewing is processed on the device. The app does not include a feature that uploads PDFs to an external server for processing.

Can I open password-protected PDFs?

When a PDF requires a user password, the app shows a password dialog. If the password unlocks the PDF, reading continues.

Are in-app purchases required?

No. Tips are optional support purchases. The app's reading features can be used without purchasing a tip.

Environment and Issues

What should I do if a PDF does not open?

Make sure the file is a PDF, is not damaged, and has fully downloaded from iCloud Drive.

What if the left and right pages are not as expected?

Check the direction, two-page view, cover display, and cover mode settings. Some PDFs may require manual adjustment depending on their metadata.

Can I reset the settings?

Yes. Choose Reset from the options in Settings to restore default display settings and close the current document.

Troubleshooting

If the app does not work as expected, check these items in order.

Restart the app

If the display temporarily breaks or the app stops responding, quit the app and open it again.

Check the PDF file

Try opening another PDF. If only one file fails, it may be damaged, not fully downloaded, or protected by a different password.

Review display settings

Changing two-page view, cover display, direction, or cover mode may improve page order and navigation.

Reset settings

Use Reset in Settings to return to defaults. The currently opened PDF will be closed after reset.

Update iOS/iPadOS and the app

Update your OS and the app to the latest versions. Older environments may have file integration or display issues.
