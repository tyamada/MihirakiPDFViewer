A viewer for right-to-left PDFs and Japanese books

Read two-page PDFs in their natural order.

MihirakiPDFViewer is a simple PDF viewer designed for right-to-left books and Japanese publications. It reads page layout and direction settings, then lets you switch smoothly between single-page and two-page viewing.

Download View Screenshots

No more confusing page order.

Standard PDF viewers do not always display right-to-left books or two-page PDFs the way you expect. MihirakiPDFViewer reads the document settings and presents pages in a flow that matches the way the book should be read.

The Problem

Right-to-left PDFs may show pages or swipe direction in the wrong order.

The Fix

The app detects direction and layout settings, then arranges spreads naturally.

Readable Detail

Zoom, search, high-quality rendering, and sharpness help with small text.

Key Features

Essential PDF reading controls in a clean, focused interface.

1

Right-to-left and left-to-right support

Switches page movement between L2R and R2L based on the PDF direction.

2

Single-page and two-page views

Read with a layout that fits the document, with cover handling when needed.

3

Search and zoom

Search text inside the PDF and pinch to zoom in on the area you need.

4

Password-protected PDFs

Open PDFs that require a user password and continue reading after unlock.
