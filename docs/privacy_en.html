Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

MihirakiPDFViewer is an app for reading PDFs comfortably on your device. This page explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

Summary

The app's basic privacy approach.

PDF content is not sent out

Display, search, page detection, and related processing for selected PDFs are handled on the device.

No ads or analytics SDKs

The app does not include third-party SDKs for advertising delivery or behavior analytics.

Purchases are handled by Apple

In-app purchases are processed through Apple's StoreKit. The app does not directly receive payment information.

Information the App Handles

Information that may be used by each feature.

PDF Files

The app opens PDF files selected with the file picker. PDF text, metadata, and page information are used for viewing features.

  • PDF files are not uploaded to an external server.
  • PDFs in iCloud Drive are loaded according to Apple's system behavior.
  • Passwords for protected PDFs are used only for unlocking the document.

App Settings

Display settings, cover settings, two-page view, app icon settings, and similar preferences may be stored on the device.

  • Settings are used to restore app behavior.
  • Settings are not sent to a developer server.

Search Terms

Text entered in PDF search is used to search within the opened PDF. Search terms are not sent to external services.

In-App Purchases

Tip purchase status may be used for post-purchase UI and app icon changes.

  • Payment information is managed by Apple.
  • The developer does not receive credit card numbers or similar payment details.

Support Inquiries

If you contact support through GitHub Issues or email, the information you send may be reviewed as needed to reply or investigate the issue.

  • Do not send PDF content or personal information unless it is necessary.
  • Device model, OS version, app version, and reproduction steps help investigation.

Sharing with Third Parties

Information is not provided to third parties except where required by law. Apple-provided features such as in-app purchases and iCloud Drive are also subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Security

PDF files and settings are handled only as needed for the app.

Information You Manage

PDF files and passwords are managed by your device, iCloud Drive, or the storage location you use. If you handle sensitive PDFs, enable device passcode, biometric authentication, and iCloud account protection.

Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary.

Notice of Changes

Important changes will be announced on this page or in the app. The latest version is always available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please contact support.

Contact Method

Use GitHub Issues or email. When posting in a public place, do not include personal information or PDF content.
